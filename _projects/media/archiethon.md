I helped run three large events, Archiethon 3, 4 and 5 for the Celeste Community on Twitch.

I was a broadcast technician for Archiethon 3, running the livestream while coordinating players, hosts, commentators and other information about the event. I also ran our archive for this event, publishing content on YouTube and making thumbnails. We raised **$10,051.74** for OutYouth.
