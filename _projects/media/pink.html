Pink was a film I made as my final project for my grade 12 cinema class. It's about a boy who wants a pink tricycle so goes on an adventure to get one himself, building up his confidence and with many lovely encounters on the way.

I'm super happy with how it came out: even if I find mistakes in it, it's all I could have hoped for out of the project. It was scripted, directed, and edited all by me alone, with some help during the production from my friends in the credits. It followed my pretty ambitious filming style being scripted in 2 writing sessions both 3 hours long, shot over three days and edited over two more months, being handed in a record breaking 4 months before deadline!
