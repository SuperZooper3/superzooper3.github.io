The Waterloo Rocketry Team goes to launch our rockets every year at a competition, and has a tradition of making vlogs of the journey.

In 2024, it was my honour to edit the 2024 Competition Vlog. I didn't attend the competition so I had to discover the footage as I edited, but it was really fun to make!

At 1 hour and 50 minutes, it's my first long-form film, and took over 150 hours to edit, with 700 clips from a pool of 37 HOURS of content, but I'm really happy with how it came out!
