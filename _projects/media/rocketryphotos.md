As a media member and then the media lead for the [Waterloo Rocketry Team](https://www.waterloorocketry.com/), I've done lots of photography for the team, for its tests, and of projects.

They were mostly taken on a Sony A6000, edited in RawTherapee, or taken with my Google Pixel 6. Find them on our [Flickr](https://www.flickr.com/photos/uwrocketry/).
