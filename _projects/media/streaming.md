From 2020 to 2022, I was an active livestreamer at [twitch.tv/superzooper3](https://www.twitch.tv/superzooper3).

I streamed mostly a platformer called Celeste, as well as other games like Hollow Knight and Minecraft.

I amassed a small community of around two hundred followers, with a regular community of chatters to spend time with during the pandemic.

I've since mostly retired from streaming, but stay in touch with many people from that era.

My proudest achievement was getting a sub 30 minute time in the Celeste Any% speedrun. You can find the clip for that [here](https://clips.twitch.tv/SlipperySplendidMushroomKreygasm-Pxae31tiWhOYIp9A).
