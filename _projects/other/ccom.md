From 2020 to 2023, I have been a restreamer (broadcast technician), commentator/host, developer, and eventually as executive producer and moderator for the [Celeste Community on Twitch.tv](https://www.twitch.tv/celestecommunity).

In these capacities, I helped run over 30 livestreams and events, showcasing talent in our community playing the Celeste video game and putting on fun tournaments. Running these streams included coordinating talent, commentary and other staff, as well as problem solving on the fly and working in a team on big projects.

As showcased on [this page of my portfolio](/projects/programming/ccom/), I helped refactor and overhaul our broadcasting infrastructure to allow the community to run new live events, with ever higher production value, and ease of use for staff.

I also gained a lot of experience in project management, community relations and growth (through managing our [YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@CelesteCommunity)), staff management and training and working with professionalism.
