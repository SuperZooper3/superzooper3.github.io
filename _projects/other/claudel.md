In my final year of high school, I was director of the Terminale (Grade 12) Committees. In this position I overlooked five committees doing everything from running events, to raising money, marketing, making a yearbook and more.

I lead a team of about 7 leaders during the year, who themselves had 50 members in their committees. Our events were a great success, we raised over ten thousand dollars, made a wonderful yearbook and run a great prom.

It taught me a lot about project management, people skills and how to make plans at a large scale.

Personally in these events, I did a lot of logistics work, as well as selling tickets and goods, and producing videos for events like the Gala des Talents Video.
