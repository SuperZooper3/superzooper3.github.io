I participated in uOttaHack 7, and our team developed Multiverse Tetris as part of an Air Traffit Control challenge.

It's a game where you control up to 6 tetris boards, simultaneously managing them to avoid crashes, in fast paced logic based action, with a basic tetris autopilot running the other boards.

[![Multiverse Tetris](/assets/img/projects/multi_tetris.png)](https://superzooper3.github.io/multiverse-tetris/)

It was pretty fun to develop, even if stressful and painful at some points (it's all **raw** JavaScript :) ). We had two first-time hackers on our team, so it was super fun to introduce them to hackathons, and to build a fun project together!

*You can play Multiverse Tetris in your browser [here](https://superzooper3.github.io/multiverse-tetris/), as well as look at the code and documentation on [GitHub](https://github.com/SuperZooper3/multiverse-tetris).*
