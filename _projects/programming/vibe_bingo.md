VibeBingo was a silly little bingo player app that I vibe coded one evening for a Software Engineering Society event I was running at the University of Waterloo. It was made with Claude Sonnet 4 for about $1.

[![VibeBingo](/assets/img/projects/vibe1.png)](https://superzooper3.github.io/vibe-bingo/)

It's pretty cool I think! It supports making your own word sets, generating unique printable bingo sheets for each player, and then running the actual bingo with cool animations and a history of which balls were drawn.

[![VibeBingo](/assets/img/projects/vibe2.png)](https://superzooper3.github.io/vibe-bingo/)
[![VibeBingo](/assets/img/projects/vibe3.png)](https://superzooper3.github.io/vibe-bingo/)

Here's my class playing Vibe Bingo!

[![VibeBingo](/assets/img/projects/vibe4.png)](https://superzooper3.github.io/vibe-bingo/)

I love vibe coding :) It's super satisfying to bring your ideas to life so fast.

*You can use Vibe Bingo for your own events in your browser [here](https://superzooper3.github.io/vibe-bingo/), as well as look at the code and documentation on [GitHub](https://github.com/SuperZooper3/vibe-bingo).*
